## 3. Commercial Licensing Notes

- **Authoritative Data Isolation**: Tenant data is strictly isolated in multi-tenant or dedicated single-tenant enterprise deployments.
- **Billing Dependencies**: Commercial billing/payment gateway integration is operated via customer ERP or invoicing connectors (Stripe / NetSuite / SAP).
- **Compliance Certification**: The software supports regulatory compliance workflows (DGCA CAR, EASA Part-M/Part-CAMO, FAA Part 91/135) without claiming certification authority.
